Get to Know Us

🏢

Organisation Overview

Thembi's Kitchen is an informal catering stall based at a local taxi rank in Daveyton. We serve convenient, everyday meals for commuters, taxi drivers, and nearby workers.

🥐

Our Daily Offerings

In the mornings, the business sells freshly baked scones for breakfast on the go. In the afternoons, it prepares full traditional plates of warm food for customers who need quick, filling meals.

🎯

Our Mission & Motivation

As a thriving local micro-enterprise that currently depends on foot traffic and our central location at the taxi rank, this website serves as our digital storefront to better connect with our community by:

  • Overcoming location limits: Allowing nearby mall workers to view food options and place orders easily.
  • Pre-ordering convenience: Saving precious time for commuters by letting them pre-order morning scones.
  • Catering opportunities: Opening online visibility to help Thembi's Kitchen attract bulk orders for local events.
